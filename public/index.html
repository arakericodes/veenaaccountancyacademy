About Us

Two decades of shaping commerce students

Veena Accountancy Academy is led by Veena Arakeri, an experienced educator who has spent the last 20 years teaching CBSE Class 11 and 12 Accountancy, Business Studies and Economics.

Our teaching focuses on building strong fundamentals, explaining every concept in simple language, and practising extensively with board-pattern questions. Each student gets personal attention so that doubts are cleared early and confidence grows steadily through the year.

  • Concept-first teaching with real-life examples
  • Complete NCERT & CBSE syllabus coverage
  • Regular tests, revision and performance tracking
